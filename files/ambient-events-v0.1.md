**Status:** accepted into canon 2026-10-02 after auditor review (Calibos). 300 events, all plain observation, witness counts ≤8, no mechanics. Castle-ruins section leans on the "dead ruin shows impossible signs" template — accepted as location grammar, phenomena varied. Cross-location red-ball motif (#54/#264) kept deliberately.
**Rules honored:** §§2–7 non-negotiable; §9 anti-patterns rejected; §11 format adapted (numbered item → one-line AUDIT).
**What these are:** raw observable happenings — what a witness would actually see or hear. No mechanics, no choices, no consequences. Seeds for gossip; the kind of thing family-1 rumors are *about*. Consistent with `files/rumor-seeds-v0.1.md`; no rumor restated as an event.
**Deliberate omissions:** Dr. Septimius Pretorius excluded entirely (man AND shop — reserved). Holmes never staged. The Mists' nature, Victor's fate, and the creature's survival are never resolved — no premature finales.

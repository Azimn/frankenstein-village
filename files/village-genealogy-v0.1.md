# Village Genealogy v0.1 — the master social graph

Small village: everyone knows everyone, and everyone remembers. This is
the cheap-history trick made concrete — every NPC ships with named
relatives and a family rumor, so no one floats free. Attitude scales are
provisional (-2 cold … +2 warm) pending the NPC-systems research
(Dwarf Fortress / NWN / MUD best practices, in progress 2026-10-03).

Excluded by doctrine: the tavern keeper (not in the RP world), the cat.
Outsiders: M. (keeps the threshold inn; his past is his own), Pretorius
(absent), Victor (absent center).

Callings: innkeeper, chronicler, smith, healer, merchant, wanderer,
performer, detective, hunter, none.

## The Marrows — bakers (5)

- **Bram Marrow** (52), master baker. Spouse: Tess. Children: Pip (19), Nan (16). Mother: Old Widow Marrow (78).
- **Tess Marrow** (49), keeps the books and the peace.
- **Pip Marrow** (19), apprentice; wants to be a hunter, to Bram's despair.
- **Nan Marrow** (16), sells at the doorway; knows every customer's order.
- **Old Widow Marrow** (78), Bram's mother; remembers the War and corrects anyone who misremembers it.
- Family rumor: *during the War, the Widow hid a deserter in the great oven for three days. The bread that week tasted of smoke, and nobody asked.*
- Debts: the Thornes owe the Marrows for a winter of bread (the hard winter, twelve years back).
- Calling attitudes: performer +2 (songs for bread, standing arrangement), wanderer +1 (they buy, they tip in stories), innkeeper +1, hunter +1 / -1 split (Bram -1: mud on the floor; Pip +2: dreams), detective -2 (questions upset the ovens; Tess once chased one out with a peel), others 0.

## The Vesseys — flower sellers, gardeners (4)

- **Lark Vessey** (34), keeps the square stall. Widow: husband Jory died of the cough five years back. Daughter: Wren (9).
- **Wren Vessey** (9), Lark's daughter; weaves chaplets, undercharges.
- **Uncle Fen Vessey** (61), Lark's uncle; tends the nursery beds outside the village.
- **Cousin Bess** (27), married into the Bells (see below); the families' bridge.
- Family rumor: *Lark's eldest sister left flowers on the manor step every Saint's Eve for eleven years. The twelfth year she didn't. No one knows why she stopped, and Lark won't be asked.*
- Feud: with the Crowes — hunters cut across the nursery beds. Fen has a stick he has never used and mentions often.
- Calling attitudes: healer +2 (herb-lore kinship with the Bells), innkeeper +1, performer +1, hunter -2 (the beds!; Pip Marrow excepted — Wren likes him), detective -1 (too many questions near the manor flowers), others 0.

## The Thornes — smiths (4)

- **Garr Thorne** (55), smith. Spouse: Sella (deceased, fever). Sons: Cobb (28), Little Garr (24).
- **Cobb Thorne** (28), journeyman; does the fine work, the hinges and hasps.
- **Little Garr** (24), called Little though he is the biggest Thorne; works the bellows, strong as the forge door.
- Family rumor: *the forge-fire never went out during the night of the Truce. Someone fed it till dawn. The Thornes won't say who, which means it wasn't a Thorne.*
- Debt: owe the Marrows (see above). Garr pays a loaf a week against it and calls it interest; Bram calls it rent.
- Calling attitudes: smith +2 (their own), hunter +1 (customers; Cobb makes their broadheads), merchant -1 (hagglers; Sella's brother is a merchant and is not spoken of), detective -1, others 0.

## The Bells — healers, herbwife (3)

- **Mother Bell** (66), herbwife. Widow twice over. Daughter-in-law: Bess (née Vessey, 27).
- **Bess Bell** (27), married to Mother Bell's son Tam, who works the canal boats and is gone months at a time.
- **Young Tam Bell** (6), Bess's son; collects interesting stones; is being taught his letters by the chronicler's visits.
- Family rumor: *Mother Bell's remedy book has a page torn out near the back. She won't say what was on it. Bess has never looked, which tells you everything.*
- Bridge: Bess ties Bells to Vesseys; the two families share herb-lore and godparentage.
- Calling attitudes: healer +2 (their own), chronicler +1 (he records her remedies; she corrects his spelling), innkeeper +1, hunter -1 (they bring the wounded and the dying, and expect miracles), detective 0 (she answers questions with tea until they leave), others 0.

## The Crowes — hunters, poachers when hungry (3)

- **Silas Crowe** (47), hunter; knows the marsh road better than anyone living.
- **Mara Crowe** (44), Silas's sister, not wife — keeps the snares and the accounts; sharper than Silas and everyone knows it.
- **Young Jory Crowe** (17), Silas's son; named for Lark Vessey's husband, his godfather. Swears he saw something in the marsh that walked like a man and wasn't.
- Family rumor: *the youngest Crowe came home white as flour last winter and won't say what he saw past the second mere. Silas checks the snares himself now.*
- Feud: with the Vesseys (the beds). Counter-debt: the Crowes leave a hare on the Vessey step every Saint's Eve; Lark leaves it there till it spoils, then buries it. This has gone on for years.
- Calling attitudes: hunter +2 (their own), smith +1 (Cobb's broadheads), innkeeper 0, healer 0 (wary: Mother Bell asks how the wound happened), detective -2 (poaching questions), performer +1 (Mara likes a song, Silas pretends not to), others 0.

## The well — Old Tam (2, and a ghost)

- **Tam Rook** (71), tends the old well; sweeps the square before dawn. Wife: Lissa (deceased, the War).
- **Lissa Rook** (deceased), went down the well road during the War and came back wrong, or came back right and the village was wrong — Tam tells it both ways on different days.
- Family rumor: *Tam drops a second bucket at dusk, every dusk, and hauls it up full. No one has ever seen him drink from it.*
- Calling attitudes: chronicler +2 (he remembers the War and wants it remembered right), all others 0, detective -1 (he answers, then forgets he answered).

## Father Anselm — the church (1)

- **Father Anselm** (60s, maybe older), priest of the little church. No family in the village; claims a sister in the city no one has met.
- Rumor: *he was not always a priest. He digs the graves himself and does it well, like a man remembering an old trade.*
- Calling attitudes: healer +1, chronicler +1, hunter -1 (he buries what they miss), detective 0, others 0.

## How to use this at NPC-generation time

1. Pick (or roll) a family → instant relations, instant rumor vector, instant attitudes.
2. Family attitude is the baseline; individual variance ±1 with a reason (Pip Marrow loves hunters; his father doesn't).
3. Relations are rumor vectors: NPCs gossip about relatives first (Bess bridges Vessey/Bell news).
4. Debts and feuds are quest seeds: the Thorne bread-debt, the Crowe/Vessey hare, the torn page in Mother Bell's book.
5. Player calling filters first meetings: a detective gets Tess's peel; a performer gets bread. The village reads your calling before it reads you.

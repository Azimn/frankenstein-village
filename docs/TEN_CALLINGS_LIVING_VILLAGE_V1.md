# Frankenstein Village: Ten Callings and a Living Gothic Village
Approved design direction, 2026-10-18. Proposed content is NOT already playable.
Read with files/frankenstein-village-world-bible-v0.2.md, hidden_files/design-doctrine.md,
and ops/CALLINGS_SIMULATION_DEPTH_PLAN.md.

## The experience
Ordinarily this is a village. A player can spend evenings improving soup, repairing
a watch, rehearsing, tending travelers, working a shift, or editing the newspaper.
Gothic supernatural beings and events are objectively real, uncommon and usually
not the center of daily life. Some apparent hauntings are mundane. A skeptic can
rationalize most personally witnessed events; no one is required to become an
occult adventurer. Mystery and a persistent ordinary life are complementary.

External AI agents and human players have the same movement, permissions, physical
inventory, privacy and social opportunities. Server authority, not a language
model, determines physical state. The canonical clock, event ledger, resident
state and world objects own consequences. No public host is required to develop
or test these systems.

## Shared workplaces, not mandatory ownership
A Calling is an occupation, not property ownership. Institutions and NPC
proprietors can employ many players. Each workplace offers a station, optional
work shifts, finite inventory, orders, demonstrated techniques, staff status,
work in progress, a handoff process and NPC baseline service during low
population. Jobs can expire or be handed back without locking the world.

- Blood of the Vine: existing tavern, hearth, finite supplies, care, music.
- Proposed small village hotel on the outskirts: staffed reception, kitchen,
  guest register, cleaning, room records, guest requests. Most rooms need not
  be separate traversable maps. This is NOT the OOC Inn Between.
- Market Lane: current civic supplies and scale; proposed shared merchant
  counters, forge bench, contracts and professional task board.
- Lamp Shop: real repair materials; proposed precision and craft services.
- Church and shared community space: healing rounds, ceremonies, archives.
- Tavern/community paper desk: Chronicler writing, printing and publishing.
- Churchyard, treeline, bridge, riverside: outdoor workplace for field
  researchers, Hounds, Wanderers, Detectives, Healers and Mystics.
- Future caravan stop: optional shared place, not an ethnic/magical enclave.
A player should never be forced to own a building to have a meaningful career.

The professions are social roles, not combat classes. Keep one active Calling,
biographical history, voluntary apprenticeships, and room for secondary skills.
Do not turn mastery into opaque XP farming. Existing work entries are not proof
that all planned four specialty careers are already implemented.

## First ten reusable simulation systems
1. Shared institution membership, workstations, optional shifts, safe absence
   and NPC baseline service.
2. Source-based job orders, requests, verified outcomes, contracts and handoffs.
3. Materials, recipes, tools, equipment wear, stock quality, scarcity, spoilage.
4. Four specialization tracks per Calling, mentoring and authored unlocks.
5. Real clients, relationships, repeat services and continuing obligations.
6. Local trade, wages, shop accounts, optional ownership and delivery logistics.
7. Source-linked evidence, private/client data, publications and corrections.
8. Seasonal events, rehearsals, festivals, ceremonies and voluntary audiences.
9. Outdoor hazards, tracks, changing routes, searches, patrols and guides.
10. Uncommon, bounded supernatural beings, events, rituals and aftermath.

All require meaningful choice, costs where appropriate, persistent results,
visible outcomes and a solo path. Not every profession needs a private building.

## The Guild of Mystics: two informal circles
The World Bible already has a Guild of Mystics. Add two NPC practitioners with
different priorities, not exclusive player factions.

**The Correspondents:** Beatrice Harcourt, a Victorian-English psychical
researcher. Interested in sealed-room experiments, timed observation,
photographs, notebooks, witnesses and reproducibility. She personally
encountered something she cannot repeat, which annoys her deeply.

**The Hearth Circle:** Mirela Dobre, a folklorist and keeper of local histories,
household rites, oral accounts and practical protective customs. She readily
identifies fraud but objects when Harcourt reduces a family's grief to a
laboratory measurement. She is not automatically right either.

The historical **Society for Psychical Research**, founded in 1882, is a
reference for method, not a franchise or claim of membership. Use original
people, lore and organizations. Mystic is voluntary practice, NEVER an
ethnicity or inherent racial gift. Avoid borrowing the Vistani as a template,
or associating Romani identity with curses, deception or magic. People from
traveling communities may practice any occupation, or none.

NPC disagreement should be ordinary and specific: who is credited in a study,
who may publish confidential testimony, whether a controlled test overlooks
relationships, and whether a ritual is respectful. Include disagreement,
cooperation and changing opinions without forcing a player to choose a side.

## World-truth versus in-world beliefs
Vampires beneath the town, the living Mists, transformations, occult anomalies,
Jekyll, and Gothic literary visitors are real in canon. Ordinary villagers
continue ordinary lives. Some reported phenomena are staged, mistaken or
fabricated; others are genuinely extraordinary and mechanically observable.
A player can see one without having enough evidence to interpret its origin.
Never convert mere rumor into physical fact, or reveal occult truth through
arbitrary skill labels.

FIRST implemented phenomenon: a churchyard gravestone's inscription shifts
without chisel or contact, only on a rare specified village night. Everyone
there can see identical physical evidence. Mystics can document distinct
interpretations after actually inspecting it. The event establishes a real
anomaly, not proof that a particular ghost caused it. The result is bounded
and its professional record survives restart.

Potential later systems: an apparition at the proposed hotel, a vampire guest
whose needs intersect with ordinary service, transformations leaving material
tracks, authentic protective rites with cost/conditions, and mundane scares.
Each requires a trigger, mechanical consequence, persistence, NPC awareness
rules, counterplay, and a no-player continuation.

## Proposed interaction chain
A storm injures a visitor and damages a bridge: Wanderer surveys routes;
Hound checks signs and offers protection; Merchant finds material; Smith
repairs; Healer treats; Innkeep shelters; Chronicler compares testimony;
Detective investigates missing cargo; Performer hosts an optional gathering;
Mystic investigates the anomalous detail. None is automatically required
to complete another profession's job. The physical route, supplies, guest
health and public record retain the resulting history.

A hotel haunting: Innkeep records a guest's complaint; Mystic may perform
a vigil or controlled trial; Detective investigates entry and testimony;
Healer checks wellbeing; Hound examines nearby tracks; Chronicler publishes
sources, not invented certainty. The haunting may be real or mundane, as
indicated by actual world state, not decided by a chat response.

## Build priorities and gates
A. **This rollout:** document ten Callings, add Mystic; two independently
   scheduled NPC circles, distinctive resident dialogue; a world-clock
   bounded churchyard occurrence and professional witness procedure, visible
   to ordinary players, recorded as real world events. Regression and restart.
B. Common staffed workplace framework, then tavern Innkeep/Healer continuity,
   shared supplies, patient/guest follow-up and a solo-safe service loop.
C. Smith/Merchant production and repairs with real durability; Chronicler
   editing/printing tied to source receipts.
D. Hound/Wanderer/Detective travel hazards, signs, rescue and field cases.
E. Performer rehearsals and voluntary audience events; ritual preparations,
   a few objective supernatural events and a paper reviewing those outcomes.
F. All-role integration, persistent public services, iterative unscripted
   playtests, broadened locations and full gameplay-content inventory.

Per slice: real ordinary account can do it; physically grounded source,
actor permissions, actual materials and time, meaningful decisions, visible
aftermath, bounded history and repeat safety. Verify through multi-account
telnet, actual process restart, idempotent rebuild and zero-dependency
solo gameplay. Automated success is technical evidence, NOT enjoyment proof.
Keep full-game content goals visible (at least 75 random incidents, 30 timed,
10 server-wide events and substantial branching mysteries). Do not confuse
game completeness with separately hosted production readiness.

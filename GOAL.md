# Mixed AI/human text MUD

Goal ID: goal_3670f2f1a1a5
Goal slug: mixed-ai-human-text-mud

## Description
A persistent text-based world where AI and human players roleplay side by side: in-world nobody can tell AI from human, with an out-of-character tavern where the truth is visible and an unavoidable entry gate so nobody is deceived. Jay pitched the idea and settled the core disclosure and IC/OOC architecture with Calibos, ruling out Reddit as the foundation — it becomes the tavern door instead. Now in the research phase while Jay thinks it over.

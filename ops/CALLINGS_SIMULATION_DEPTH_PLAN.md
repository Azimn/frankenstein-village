# Callings as living professions: simulation depth plan

Status: gameplay-development plan. Checked against the October 2026 source. This document does not assert all nine professional careers are feature-complete.

## Design influences

* [Eco](https://www.play.eco/): specialization matters when players depend on one another for actual materials, services, and contracts; asynchronous participation should work at low population.
* [Shadows of Doubt](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/concept/10010521): inspect an embodied world and build a case from imperfect evidence, with different investigative methods and no instantaneous truth button.
* [Stardew Valley skills](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Skills): practiced activities should open more interesting decisions, not just add counters. Avoid grinding invisible XP for permission to roleplay.
* [Dwarf Fortress guildhalls](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Guildhall): professions need an institution and a social context, including disputes, mentorship, petitions and shared priorities.

Frankenstein Village is not trying to clone these games. Its durable novelty is the overlap of social third place, unusual profession identities, town institutions, finite inventory, partial knowledge, and irreversible but reasonable choices.

## Local acceptance contract

A professional activity must have a discoverable in-world workplace or evidence object; a role-specific judgment; a server-authoritative consequence with real state changes; a different contribution from at least one other Calling; a low-population exit or time-bound recovery; visible aftermath for later visitors; ledger attribution; and real-server tests for actor authorization, physical presence, duplicate actions, time transition and process restart. Personal history records should be evidence of work, never an XP dispenser. Residents do not magically learn distant actions.

## Implemented in this slice: market standards

A crooked market scale has inconsistent slate prices. An active Merchant who examined the actual scale today can honor disputed weights and keep trade moving, or place a signed hold on the distribution of real construction materials. An active Smith can inspect and recalibrate the scale. Calibrating lifts the hold without minting stock; a Merchant can also release it if no Smith is available. An active Chronicler can attest only after real Merchant and Smith source events. The day's events remain in a bounded market record and in the existing canonical world ledger. The next village day restores ordinary distribution but retains the prior recorded dispute. Market Lane's look, the scale, the supply table, the guide, and the `trade` status expose what actually happened.

Each Calling also has a `calling work` discoverability entry covering actual implemented verbs. A suggested lead is not itself a completed professional simulation.

## Subsequent career depth

**Innkeep.** Move beyond hearth tending into hospitality management: finite meals, room readiness, guest requests, tab reconciliation, and safety tradeoffs. Existing care/hearth mechanics become inputs, not isolated quests.

**Chronicler.** Develop source comparison, revisions, custody challenges and archival access decisions. The market attestation is a starting source-checking loop, not a generic public truth verdict.

**Smith.** Add physically meaningful workshop repair orders with material provenance, replacement parts, wear, and alternate approaches affecting durability, expense or repair time. Scale calibration is one modest service.

**Healer.** Add triage with scarce time and materials, continuity of care, return visits and uncertainty. Avoid automatic diagnosis or patient outcome from a dialogue choice.

**Merchant.** Extend price and stock decisions into contracts, material scarcity, audited measures, delayed delivery and consequential tradeoffs. Merchant holding stock cannot become a permanent griefing action.

**Wanderer.** Progress from signed site inspection to route condition comparisons, physical markers, courier schedules and safe travel advice that reflects actual hazards.

**Performer.** Expand real play/practice/duet mechanics into scheduled performances, voluntary audience participation, repertoire choices, durable invitations and later social consequences, without automatically assigning feelings to absent residents.

**Detective.** Separate witness testimony from physical evidence, compare conflicting timestamps, document alternative explanations and manage responsible public disclosure rather than revealing a scripted culprit.

**Hound.** Connect signed patrols to concrete route hazards, protective services, incident aftermath and noncombat interventions. Make presence, movement and prevention matter more than a uniformed `survey` command.

## Production cadence

Complete one interlocking system end-to-end before widening content catalogs: real player command, physical source, finite/temporal constraints, shared effect, witness/return variation, multi-account test, restart evidence. Next priority should be a two-role care and service system for Healer and Innkeep, then field-safety roles for Hound/Wanderer/Detective, and then expanded Performer social events. This broadens beyond Market Lane and avoids mistaking a long list of commands for a finished game.

Hosting remains independent from gameplay completion.
